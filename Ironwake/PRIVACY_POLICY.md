## Privacy Policy for Ironwake

Ironwake ("the game") is developed and published by **Seahorse Games** ("we", "us").
This policy explains how the game handles your information. In short: **the game does
not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

### Information we collect
**None.** Ironwake does not collect any personal or sensitive user data. The game:

- Does **not** require an account, login, or registration.
- Does **not** collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, or device identifiers.
- Does **not** include analytics, advertising, or tracking software.
- Does **not** make network connections to transmit user data.

### Game save data
Your progress (characters, items, companions, settings) is stored **only on your own
device**, in the app's private local storage. This data never leaves your device, is not
transmitted to us or any third party, and is removed if you uninstall the game.

### Data sharing
We do **not** share any user data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

### Children's privacy
The game does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

### Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised
"Last updated" date.
