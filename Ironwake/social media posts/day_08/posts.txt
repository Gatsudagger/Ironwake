DAY 8
========================================

--- Post 1: The camp ---
Between runs you're at camp.

Petra sells supplies. Dorn forges. Vex trains you. Maren works runes. Sable brews. Vael changes how you look. Bairc keeps your creatures.

Every one of them remembers what you've done for them.
[image: post_1.png  (in this folder)]

--- Post 2: Bonds with the camp ---
Help the camp and the camp helps back.

Gifts and favors deepen your bond with each person. Rank up their stations and they open new services - better stock, cheaper training, new crafts.
[image: post_2.png  (in this folder)]

--- Post 3: The tavern board ---
The tavern board is where the townsfolk ask for things.

Hunts, errands, favors. Pick up a posting before a run and there's a little extra waiting when you get back.
[image: post_3.png  (in this folder)]
